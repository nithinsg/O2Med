Finalist 1 · recommended
umedha
oo-MAY-dha · उमेधा

Umeed (उम्मीद) is Hindi for hope. Medha (मेधा) is Sanskrit for intelligence. Folded together, with med sitting in the middle, they make a word that has never existed and still means something to 600 million people the first time they hear it.

The mark is a lowercase u that is also a pair of cupped hands, holding a diya flame. The lamp is India's oldest sign of hope, and of knowledge pushing back the dark. It works at 16 pixels and moulded into white plastic.

Why these colours: every rival sits in hospital blue or teal. Kohl and Dawn feel like a home at first light, warm and calm, and still serious enough for a pulmonologist's desk. The flame colour is used small and rarely, so it stays special. Type: Bricolage Grotesque for the name and headlines, Figtree for text, Mukta for Hindi.

Finalist 2
medswara
med-SWAA-ra · मेडस्वरा

Swara is a musical note in Indian music, and in yoga it is the flow of breath through the body. Medswara reads as "medicine in tune with you": care that listens to each person's own rhythm, which is exactly what an AI that learns a patient's patterns does.

The mark is an S drawn as a breath, with the sound passing through it and the anusvara dot above, the sign that makes a note ring on.

Finalist 3
medaangan
med-AAN-gun · मेडआंगन

The aangan is the open courtyard at the heart of an Indian home, where the family gathers, the sun comes in and the elders sit. Medaangan says medicine at the centre of the home.

The mark is a courtyard seen from above: the walls of the house, an open doorway, and the sun in the middle.

What I'd do

Choose Umedha. Put everything else under it.

It is the only finalist that hides "med" inside a brand-new word, carries both hope and intelligence, says nothing about lungs, and still sounds like a global consumer-health company next to Oura or Withings.

How it sounds in a sentence

"My father's on Umedha." "Ask Umedha to send the Aangan team." "Your Sense report shows three hours a night." Short, possessive, easy to repeat.

Thoraq and Respeon

You liked both. Keep them in reserve as names for a future respiratory or cardiopulmonary product line under Umedha, and decide once the trademark search is back.

Before you commit

Buy umedha.com, .in and .health today (about ₹3,000 in all) so nobody else can. Then have a trademark attorney search India in classes 10, 44, 35, 9 and 42. Run a five-minute test: say the name to ten patients' families and ten doctors, and ask them to spell it the next day.
