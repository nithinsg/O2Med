### 3 Working principle

### 3.1Working principle

Using the principle of molecular sieve pressure swing adsorption, 90% to 96%
V/V) of oxygen can be obtained by adsorbing nitrogen and other gas components in
the air.

When the equipment is working, compressed air is injected into a closed
adsorption tower equipped with molecular sieves, causing the pressure in the
adsorption tower to rise. The molecular sieves in the adsorption tower absorb a large
amount of nitrogen in the compressed air as the ambient pressure increases, and the
nitrogen in the compressed air The oxygen still exists in the form of gas and is
collected through certain pipelines. This process is often called the "adsorption"
process. When the nitrogen adsorbed by the molecular sieve in the container reaches
the critical adsorption saturation state, the adsorption tower is blown and depressurized.
As the ambient pressure decreases, the molecular sieve's ability to adsorb nitrogen
decreases, and the nitrogen is released back to the gas phase from inside the molecular
sieve and is discharged as waste gas. . This process is often called "desorption." In
order to ensure the continuous and stable output of oxygen, the oxygen concentrator
usually uses two (or more) molecular sieve adsorption towers, which are controlled by
a control valve so that one adsorption tower is in the adsorption process while the
other adsorption tower is in the desorption process. Alternating work completes the
continuous oxygen production process; when the user inhales, the micro-pressure
sensor in the machine detects the inhalation signal, opens the solenoid valve, and
delivers the oxygen in the gas tank to the user through the nasal oxygen tube.

### 6 Cleaning, maintenance and maintenance

In the normal state and the single fault state, the part of the gas passing through
the oxygen concentrator that may be contaminated by body fluids or exhaled air is
only the nasal oxygen tube.

Warning: Do not perform maintenance and maintenance during the operation of the
oxygen concentrator.

⚠ **Warning:** not to disassemble the equipment, nor do you try to perform any
maintenance other than the tasks described in this manual. Disassembly will cause the
risk of electric shock and void your warranty; for incidents other than those described
in this manual, please contact the equipment supplier and the authorized personnel will
carry out repairs.

⚠ **Warning:** Only use spare parts recommended by the manufacturer to ensure normal
function and avoid the risk of fire and burns.

Regular visual inspections of the equipment are required to ensure that the exposed
parts are not significantly damaged. Typical visual inspections include：

a) The battery connector should not be bent or deformed.

b) The oxygen outlet is not loose.

c) The shell should be completely in place and fixed, without cracks or other
visible damage.

d) The air intake filter should be installed in place without debris, dust or other
obstacles.

⚠ **Warning:** Pay attention to cleaning, and make sure that the equipment is fully dried
before it can be used. It should be stored in a dry and dust-free environment. If used by
different patients, please clean the equipment and

replace the registered nasal oxygen tube to prevent the risk of cross-infection.

a) Remove the air intake filter from the air intake end of the oxygen concentrator.

thoroughly before use. To purchase a new air intake filter, please contact the
equipment supplier.

#### 6.5 Filter cotton cleaning

Dust may cause clogging of the filter cotton and affect the normal operation of
the oxygen concentrator; the cleaning and replacement of the filter cotton is very
important to protect the compressor and molecular sieve and extend the life of the
oxygen concentrator. Please clean and replace in time.

⚠ **Warning:** If the filter cotton is not installed or the filter cotton is not completely
dried and loaded into the oxygen concentrator, it will cause permanent damage to the
oxygen concentrator.

cotton.

once a month; after loading the filter cotton, cover the filter cotton cover and use a
phillips screwdriver to screw back the fixing screws.
