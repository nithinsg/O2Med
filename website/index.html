The harms of hypoxia.

01

Lowers metabolic efficiency and immunity, leaving you prone to colds.

02

Insomnia, slower reactions and memory decline.

03

Cor pulmonale, worsened hypertension and arrhythmia.

04

Can trigger myocardial infarction and cerebral thrombosis.

05

Liver dysfunction, liver fibrosis and kidney insufficiency.

06

Long-term hypoxia leads to declining body function, a vicious circle.

Pulse or Active. Dual mode, freely switchable.

Pulse mode

Oxygen on demand

Oxygen when you breathe in, paused when you breathe out. Made for people with mild to moderate hypoxia who need to go out. Stride 2 detects your inhalation and releases high-concentration oxygen from its storage tank.

Active mode

Fast oxygen output

For altitude sickness, palpitations, chest tightness and other acute hypoxia. A fixed rate of 20 pulses a minute, and if your breath is too weak to detect, this mode starts automatically.
