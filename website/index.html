Mode

  1. Push the nasal cannula tube onto the grey O₂ outlet beside the screen. Loop the tubing over both ears and rest the prongs just inside your nostrils.

  2. Press the round screen once. It lights up, runs a quick self-check and starts delivering oxygen.

  3. Turn the chrome ring to choose the setting your doctor prescribed. The big number on screen shows it. Try the Flow buttons on the model.

  4. Pulse gives oxygen as you breathe in. Active gives a steady 20 pulses a minute for altitude sickness or chest tightness, and starts on its own if your breath is too light to sense.

  5. Plug in a Type-C cable from the wall charger, the car charger or a power bank. About 2.5 hours on a full 4-cell battery at setting 1.

  6. Press the release button on the side and slide the battery base off. Click a charged one into place and keep going.

  7. Air enters and heat leaves through the side vents. In its carry bag, keep this side uncovered so Stride 2 stays cool and quiet.

The harms of hypoxia.
