Follows your breathing

A pressure sensor detects the start of each breath and fires oxygen in the first part of it, where it reaches the lungs.

Swap the battery, keep going

The 95 Wh battery slides out and charges on its own. Carry a spare to double your time out.

Change the sieve yourself

The sieve cartridge, the part that wears out, lifts out without tools. No week-long service trip.

Home, car or battery

Runs on 100 to 240 V mains, a car socket, or the battery, and charges while it runs.

Tells you when something's wrong

Alarms for low oxygen purity, no breath detected, low battery, overheating and power faults.

Cleared to fly

The battery is under the 100 Wh airline limit. Check with your airline before you travel.

How it works

It makes oxygen from the air around you.

Draw in room air

A quiet, oil-free compressor pulls air through dust and fine filters.

Hold back the nitrogen

Under pressure, beads of zeolite (a mineral sieve) trap nitrogen and let oxygen through. Two beds take turns, so the flow never stops.

Deliver it as you breathe in

An oxygen sensor checks purity, and the machine releases a pulse into your nasal cannula at the start of each breath.

Questions

What people ask us.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Oxygen is a treatment, and too much or too little can harm. We supply Stride against a doctor's oxygen prescription, and our therapist sets it up to match.

Can I use Stride while I sleep?

Pulse dosing needs to sense your breathing, and breathing is shallower in sleep. Many people use a home concentrator with continuous flow at night. Your doctor will decide; we can supply both.

Can I take it on a flight?

Stride 1's battery is 95 Wh, under the 100 Wh limit most airlines set. Airlines have their own approval process for oxygen concentrators, so contact yours before you book, and carry enough battery for the whole journey plus delays.

Will it work in Ladakh or on the Char Dham route?

Stride 1 is rated down to an air pressure of 56 kPa, about 4,700 m. That covers Leh, Kedarnath and most passes, but not Khardung La. Use the altitude explorer above, and check with your doctor before the trip.

What happens if it stops working?

Call or message us. In our launch cities a working replacement reaches you within 48 hours, and we repair yours in the meantime. Every Stride has a three-year warranty.

Where is Stride made?

Stride 1 and Stride 2 are made for ThoraQx by Sichuan Jiayi Medical Device Co., Ltd. in Chengdu, China, a licensed manufacturer. We test, set up, service and support them in India. Our own India-designed Stride is in development.

Why do other brands say "5 litres" for a portable?

Portables don't put out 5 litres a minute. The figure is a marketing "equivalent". We publish the real oxygen output in ml per minute so you and your doctor can compare honestly.
